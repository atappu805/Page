Google Drive API — User Data & Limited Use Policy

PixelMusic's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Specific Purpose of Access

PixelMusic requests the read-only scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly) solely to allow users to play and stream their own audio files directly from Google Drive.

2. How Your Data Is Handled

  • Read-Only File Inspection: PixelMusic inspects file names and MIME types to display audio files (.mp3, .flac, .m4a, .wav, .ogg, .opus) and folders in the user's Drive.
  • No Modifying or Uploading: PixelMusic never uploads files, modifies content, or deletes files from your Google Drive.
  • On-Device Token Storage: Google OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device in secure Android DataStore storage. They are never sent to external servers or third parties.
  • Direct Streaming: Audio streaming occurs directly between Google's media servers and the local ExoPlayer engine on your Android device.

3. Disconnecting Access

You can revoke PixelMusic's access to your Google Drive at any time inside the app by tapping Disconnect in the Google Drive screen, or via your Google Account Security Settings.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

PixelMusic is developed as an open-source, privacy-first Android application. This policy details how data is handled.

1. Information Collection

PixelMusic does not collect, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising identifiers, or behavioral logging tools.

2. Local Device Permissions

  • Audio Files & Media: Used exclusively to index, sort, and play local music files on your device.
  • Notifications & Foreground Service: Used to display persistent playback controls and album artwork while audio is playing.
  • Internet Permission: Used solely to stream audio requested by the user from external sources (such as Google Drive or online audio streams).

3. Open Source Verification

PixelMusic's complete codebase is publicly accessible and auditable on GitHub at github.com/Saurav-02/PixelMusic.

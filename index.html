YouTube Music & WebView Policy

PixelMusic provides an interface for accessing YouTube Music through an Android WebView. When you sign in, browse, or play content, your interactions with YouTube Music are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

1. Authentication & Security

Sign-in is handled through the YouTube Music website displayed within PixelMusic's WebView. Your authentication is processed by Google through its services. PixelMusic is designed to use the WebView's session mechanisms rather than requiring you to enter your Google password into a separate PixelMusic login form. Please review Google's privacy policies to understand how Google processes your account information and activity.

2. How Your Data Is Handled

  • YouTube Music Activity: Playback history, liked songs, recommendations, and other account activity may be associated with your Google account and handled according to Google's settings and policies.
  • Network Communications: Requests required for authentication, browsing, and playback are handled by YouTube Music and its supporting services. Depending on the implementation, PixelMusic may also process technical information needed to operate the app.
  • Session Management: WebView cookies and other session data may be stored on your device to maintain your sign-in. You can clear available WebView data through PixelMusic's settings, where supported. Doing so may sign you out or reset website preferences.

3. Disconnecting Access

You can sign out through YouTube Music where supported, or clear the relevant WebView data in PixelMusic's settings. You can also review your Google Account's security settings to manage account access and connected devices. Clearing app data may remove locally stored session information, but it does not necessarily delete activity already associated with your Google account.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

PixelMusic is developed as an open-source, privacy-first Android application. This policy details how data is handled.

1. Information Collection

PixelMusic does not collect, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising identifiers, or behavioral logging tools.

2. Local Device Permissions

  • Audio Files & Media: Used exclusively to index, sort, and play local music files on your device.
  • Notifications & Foreground Service: Used to display persistent playback controls and album artwork while audio is playing.
  • Internet Permission: Used solely to stream audio requested by the user from external sources (such as YouTube Music or online audio streams).

3. Open Source Verification

PixelMusic's complete codebase is publicly accessible and auditable on GitHub at github.com/Saurav-02/PixelMusic.

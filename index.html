How PixelMusic Accesses Your Google Drive

PixelMusic requests read-only permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly) strictly to enable cloud music streaming for individual users. Here is our solemn commitment:

  • Local Read Only: PixelMusic only reads file metadata (names, sizes, MIME types) to locate audio files (MP3, FLAC, M4A, WAV, etc.) in your Drive.
  • No File Uploads or Modifications: PixelMusic never uploads, alters, deletes, or renames any of your Drive files.
  • Zero Data Collection or Sharing: Your Google Drive tokens, directory listings, and audio content never leave your Android device. They are never sent to external servers, trackers, or third parties.
  • Local On-Device Streaming: Audio streams directly between Google's media servers and your local ExoPlayer audio engine on your phone.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

PixelMusic is a free, open-source audio player developed with privacy as a foundational principle. We believe user data belongs to the user alone.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PixelMusic does not collect, log, track, sell, or monetize any personal identification information. There are no tracking SDKs, no behavioral telemetry, and no third-party advertising networks embedded in the app.

2. Device & Cloud Storage Permissions

  • Audio / Storage Permission: Used strictly to index and play local music files stored on your device.
  • Google Drive Read Access: Requested optionally when you connect Google Drive to stream your personal audio files. Stored tokens are held securely in your private device DataStore and can be deleted instantly by tapping "Disconnect" inside the app.

3. Open Source Transparency

The entire source code of PixelMusic is publicly auditable on GitHub at github.com/atappu805/Clean.
